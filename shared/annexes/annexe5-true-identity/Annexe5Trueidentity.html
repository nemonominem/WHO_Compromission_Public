Annexe 4 - True identity of  小山狗 (Little Mountain Dog):

Who is Little Mountain Dog?

小山狗 = Xiǎoshān Gǒu, is a nickname for Xuejing Gou (苟雪静), an employee of Vision Medicals (广州  微远基因 科技有限公司, Chinese: Guangzhou Weiyuan Gene Technology Co., Ltd). Email: xjgou@visionmedicals.com (most likely, no bounce back).

She was likely born in the early 1990s, is very cultured and sensitive and a film lover. Pictures of her can be found here.

  • 2018 or  2019: Attested at BGI-Shenzhen (maybe an internship), Shenzhen 518083, China. See this paper (publication date may be after she moved to Vision Medicals).
